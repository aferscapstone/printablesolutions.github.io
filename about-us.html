Our story

Built to fix a supply problem

Cebu Printable Solutions Corporation was founded in May 2014 in Cebu City, in direct response to a gap in the market. Advertising companies across Cebu were consistently let down by Manila-based suppliers — undersized tarpaulins and unreliable inventory that left projects stalled.

We began by supplying tarpaulins alone. Since then, we have grown into a full line of advertising materials, including Self-Adhesive Vinyl, Acrylic Sheets, and PVC Boards, alongside supporting accessories such as eyelet punchers, eyelets, glue, X-Banner stands, and roll-up banner stands.

Every product is imported directly from established partner factories that meet our quality standards, and every roll carries our own brand assurance, Printable Solutions.

Over the past decade, we have earned recognition as a trusted name in advertising materials across the Philippines — Luzon, Visayas, and Mindanao. Strict quality control ensures every tarpaulin meets the required weight and thickness, and every product we carry is priced competitively without compromising on quality.

New in 2026

Our entry into machinery

In early 2026, our distributorship agreement with Avery Dennison came to a close. Rather than stand still, we treated it as an opportunity to broaden our business and better serve the industry we have supported for over a decade.

That opportunity led us into machinery. After more than a decade supplying the materials behind every print job, we are now bringing in the machines that produce them — a natural next step in offering our customers a more complete solution.

We are proud to introduce Audley to the Philippine market — our first machinery brand, backed by the same commitment to quality and reliable supply that our customers have trusted since 2014.
