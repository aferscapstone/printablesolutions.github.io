FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Do you offer wholesale or bulk discounts?

Yes! We specialize in direct wholesale pricing for printing businesses, sign makers, and corporate clients. Contact our sales team or request a quote for bulk orders.

Where are your physical branches located?

Our main hub is in Mandaue City, Cebu, and we also have a branch in Pasig City, Metro Manila for Luzon-based clients and quick pickups.

What types of raw materials do you supply?

We supply a wide range of advertising and printing materials, including Tarpaulin & Flex, PVC Boards (Xintra), Acrylic Sheets, Vinyl Stickers, Photo Papers, Sublimation Papers, and Avery Dennison products.

How fast is order processing and delivery?

For standard stock items, we offer rapid dispatch (typically within 24-48 hours). Delivery times may vary depending on your location and order volume.
